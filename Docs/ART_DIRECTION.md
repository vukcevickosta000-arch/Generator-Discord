# Art Direction

## 1. Visual language

**Dark, painterly, readable.** Velmoragh is a drowned vampire capital: black stone, rusted iron, blood-lacquered
wood, bone and candle-gold under a crimson moon. The battlefield must stay legible from a MOBA camera at 30+ m.

| Rule | Why |
|---|---|
| Value first: ground is mid-dark; units are brighter; spell effects are brightest (bloom above ~1.0) | Readability of the playfield |
| Team colours: **Dawn = ivory + gold + warm light**, **Dusk = obsidian + crimson + red glow** | Instant team read, even in fog |
| Silhouettes by role: melee creeps with shields, ranged with bows/crossbows, siege as carts, heroes 1.2–1.4× creep height with distinct weapons | Recognition at a glance |
| Saturated colour is reserved for magic, blood and team accents | Keep noise down |
| Faction motifs: Court = gothic filigree and blood-red; Legion = bone and verdigris; Covenant = antler, bark, silver moon; Dawnguard = sunburst and white stone | Faction identity |

**Original work only.** Nothing is traced from or modelled on existing MOBA assets; HoN is a feel reference for
camera, pacing and HUD density, never for art.

## 3. UI

- **Toolkit.** UI Toolkit (USS in `Client/Assets/Resources/UI/Styles/bloodfall.uss`).
- **Textures.** Generated by `Tools/art/generate_ui.py` (embossed iron frames, bronze ornaments, blood-lacquer
  buttons, bars, cursors, rank emblems, faction crests, logo). Nine-slice sizes are in `Textures/UI/slices.json`.
- **Fonts (OFL):**
  - *Cinzel Decorative*: display and logo.
  - *Marcellus SC*: titles and buttons.
  - *Alegreya Sans / SC*: body and labels.
  - *Pirata One*: gothic accents.
- **HUD layout (HoN-like density).**
  - Bottom centre plate: portrait, HP/mana/XP bars, ability row, stats, inventory and gold.
  - Minimap in a bottom corner (setting chooses the side).
  - Top bar: score, clock, day/night, portraits.
  - Kill feed at the top right.
  - Announcer banner at upper centre.

## 4. World and characters

| Asset class | Current state | Final target |
|---|---|---|
| Terrain | Generated heightfield (0.5 m) with 8 procedural tiling layers (`Tools/art/generate_terrain.py`): grass/moss, dirt, cobble road, rock, blood mud, bone ash, leaf litter, sun paving. Height-blended with slope rock. | Hand-painted or photogrammetry-based layer sets with normal/mask maps at the same layer slots |
| Trees | Procedural black pines and dead trees, GPU-instanced, wind in shader | Blender-modelled variants (`Models/Trees`) with leaf cards |
| Props | Procedural stand-ins for every dressing type | Blender models (`Models/Props/<type>.fbx`) |

## 5. Blender pipeline (target spec for `Blender/scripts`)

**Units and orientation.**

- 1 Blender unit = 1 m.
- Characters face **−Y in Blender**, which exports as +Z forward in Unity (`bake_axis_conversion`).
- The pivot is at the feet.

**Scale.** Hero ≈ 1.8–2.2 m, creep ≈ 1.5–1.9 m, tower ≈ 9 m, core ≈ 8 m.

**Rig.** Generic armature: `root > hips > spine > chest > neck > head`, plus arms, legs and a `weapon_r` bone.

**Required empties:**

- `projectile_origin` (ranged units)
- `overhead` (optional)

**Clips.** Unity reads action names after the `|`:

| Clip | Notes |
|---|---|
| `Idle` | Loop |
| `Run` | Loop |
| `Attack1`, `Attack2` | Impact at 40% of the clip |
| `Cast1`, `Cast2`, `Cast3`, `CastUlt` | Impact at 40% |
| `Channel` | Loop |
| `Stun` | Loop |
| `Death` | — |
| `Spawn` | Optional |

**Materials.**

- PBR base colour, normal and mask (R metal, G AO, A smoothness).
- A material with `team` in its name gets tinted to the team colour at runtime.
- Emissive parts use a separate material with emission.

**Export.** FBX: `Client/Assets/Resources/Models/<modelKey>.fbx`, with props under `Models/Props/<type>.fbx`.
`AssetImportRules` applies generic rigs and loop flags automatically.

**Budgets:**

| Asset | Triangles | Textures |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | ≤ 18k | 2048² |
| Creep | ≤ 5k | 1024² |
| Structure | ≤ 12k | 2048² |
| Prop | ≤ 3k | 512–1024² |

## 6. Icons and portraits

**Current state.** Icons are generated by `Tools/art/generate_icons.py` from the game data: a keyword-selected vector
glyph, embossed in bone, gold or steel over a theme-coloured smoky background. Portraits are placeholder
silhouettes. The spec below is for final art.

- **Sizes.** Abilities and statuses 128×128, items 128×108, portraits 256×320.
- **Painting.** Painted on dark backgrounds with a strong central silhouette; the frame comes from the UI.
- **Paths.** Filenames equal the `icon` / `portrait` keys in data, under `Resources/Textures/Icons/{Abilities,Items,Statuses,Portraits}`.

## 7. Lighting

- **Day.** Overcast pale-gold key light (intensity 1.35), warm trilight ambient, fog from 34 m.
- **Night.** Cold blue moonlight (0.75) with a crimson rim from the moon, darker ambient, fog from 26 m.
- **Transition.** Blended over 6 s.
- **Light sources.** Braziers, bone braziers, shops and the seals emit (bloom). Up to 28 real-time point lights on
  the map; the rest are emissive meshes with a ground glow decal.
- **Post.** ACES, bloom, vignette, slight lift/gamma/gain split (cool shadows, warm highlights), light film grain.
